About This Portfolio

Technology has become an important part of everyday life. It affects how people communicate, study, work, entertain themselves, and interact with society.

This portfolio presents my personal experiences and reflections as a student living in the Digital Age. It also demonstrates my understanding of the responsibilities that come with being a digital citizen.
