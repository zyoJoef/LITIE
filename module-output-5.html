Technology, People, and the Future of Society

Module 5: Technology, Work, and Human Relationships

Technology continues to change how people work, communicate, and participate in society. One of the most significant developments is the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and workplace automation. These technologies can perform certain tasks, assist employees, and help organizations become more efficient.

However, technological progress also raises important questions about employment, equal opportunities, workers' rights, and human relationships. This webpage examines how AI and automation affect employment and how society can make these technologies more inclusive and human-centered.

What Is AI and Workplace Automation?

Artificial Intelligence refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, processing information, generating content, and assisting with decisions.

Workplace automation uses technology to perform repetitive or structured tasks with less manual effort. Examples include automated customer service, AI-assisted writing, data processing, recruitment tools, and automated manufacturing equipment.

Real-World Applications

01

Customer Service

AI chatbots answer common customer questions and provide assistance at different times of the day.

02

Office Administration

Automated systems help organize documents, summarize information, and process routine administrative tasks.

03

Manufacturing

Robots and automated machines assist with assembly, inspection, and repetitive production activities.

04

Recruitment

Some organizations use software to organize applications and assist with reviewing job candidates.

Positive Effects of AI and Automation

AI and automation can improve working conditions and help organizations perform tasks more efficiently when they are properly implemented.

01. Increased Productivity

Automated systems can perform repetitive tasks quickly, allowing employees to focus on more complex and meaningful responsibilities.

02. Improved Accuracy

Properly designed systems can help process large amounts of information and reduce certain types of manual errors. Human checking is still important.

03. New Career Opportunities

The growth of AI creates demand for skills and jobs involving software development, data analysis, cybersecurity, and AI system management.

04. Assistance with Difficult Tasks

Technology can support workers by providing information, identifying patterns, and helping with tasks that require processing large amounts of data.

What Does the Research Say?

According to the International Labour Organization's 2025 research, approximately one in four workers worldwide is in an occupation with some exposure to generative AI. However, the study explains that transformation of jobs is more likely than complete replacement for most workers.

Source: International Labour Organization (2025)

Negative Effects and Challenges

Despite its advantages, AI and workplace automation can create challenges for employees, businesses, and communities.

01. Job Displacement

Some tasks previously performed by employees may become automated. Workers whose responsibilities are heavily affected may need to find new roles or learn new skills.

02. Skills Gap

Employees who do not have access to digital education or training may struggle to adapt to workplaces that increasingly use advanced technologies.

03. Workplace Inequality

Organizations and individuals with greater access to technology may gain more advantages than workers and businesses with limited resources.

04. Reduced Human Interaction

Excessive dependence on automated communication and decision-making may reduce personal interaction, teamwork, and direct relationships between employees and customers.

Ethical Concerns

AI systems may produce unfair results when their training data contains bias. For example, an automated recruitment system could disadvantage certain applicants if it relies on biased historical information.

Another concern is accountability. If an automated system makes an incorrect or unfair decision, organizations must remain responsible for investigating and correcting the problem. Human judgment should not be completely removed from important employment decisions.

Who Benefits and Who Might Be Disadvantaged?

The effects of workplace technology are not equal for everyone. Some groups may benefit from improved productivity, while others may experience difficulties adapting to technological changes.

Who Benefits?

  • Businesses: May reduce operating costs and improve productivity.
  • Technology Professionals: May gain opportunities in AI development, programming, and cybersecurity.
  • Digitally Skilled Workers: Can use AI tools to improve their performance and work efficiency.
  • Customers: May receive faster services and assistance.

Who Might Be Disadvantaged?

  • Workers in Highly Automated Roles: May experience reduced demand for certain tasks.
  • Low-Skilled Workers: May need additional training to adapt to new technology.
  • Small Businesses: May struggle with the cost of adopting advanced systems.
  • People with Limited Digital Access: May have fewer opportunities to benefit from technology.

My Analysis

I believe the benefits of AI should not be limited to large companies or people who already have advanced technical skills. Workers should have opportunities to learn and adapt instead of being left behind. Businesses should also consider the effects of automation on employees before replacing human responsibilities with machines.

Recommendations for Inclusive and Human-Centered Technology

Technology should support people and improve their quality of life rather than create unnecessary inequality or remove human dignity from the workplace.

01

Provide Accessible Training

Schools, companies, and governments should offer affordable digital skills programs so workers can learn how to use new technologies.

02

Keep Human Oversight

Important decisions involving hiring, employee evaluation, and workplace opportunities should include meaningful human review.

03

Protect Workers' Rights

Organizations should communicate how automation affects employees and provide fair opportunities for retraining and career development.

04

Ensure Fairness and Transparency

AI systems should be evaluated for bias, and organizations should explain how automated decisions are made and who is accountable.

05

Protect Privacy and Personal Data

Employers should collect only necessary employee information, protect it properly, and explain how workplace data is used.

06

Preserve Human Relationships

Technology should assist communication and collaboration rather than completely replace meaningful conversations, teamwork, and empathy.

Final Reflection

Technological advancement can improve people's lives by making work more efficient, providing new career opportunities, and helping people complete tasks that require significant time and effort. However, I believe that technology should not be considered successful only because it makes organizations faster or more productive. Its effects on workers, families, communities, and human relationships should also be considered.

To prevent technology from weakening human dignity and opportunities, companies and governments should provide accessible training, protect workers' rights, and ensure that automated systems are fair and transparent. People should also have opportunities to question important decisions made with the help of AI. Human judgment, empathy, and accountability must remain important.

As a student preparing for a future career in technology, I believe that learning new digital skills is important, but technical knowledge alone is not enough. I must also understand how technology affects other people and use my skills responsibly. My goal is to support technological progress while respecting people's rights, relationships, and opportunities.

My Personal Commitment

I will view technology as a tool that should empower people, not replace their value. I will continue developing my technical skills while practicing fairness, empathy, respect, and responsibility in the digital workplace.
